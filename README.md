# Nexora Retail Intelligence Engine (NRIE)

**Smart India Hackathon (SIH) | Problem Statement ID: 26179**

**Team Name:** Nexora  
**Project Title:** AI-Powered Retail Intelligence Platform  
**Domain:** Edge AI / Computer Vision / Retail Automation & Analytics

---

## Executive Summary

**Nexora Retail Intelligence Engine (NRIE)** is an edge-first AI platform designed for retail outlets in India (ranging from Kirana stores to large supermarkets). By deploying lightweight computer vision models directly on edge devices (RPI/NVIDIA Jetson/PC Edge Nodes), NRIE delivers:

1. **Shopper Analytics:** Footfall counter, zone traffic heatmaps, and product dwell-time measurement.
2. **Inventory Visibility:** Automated shelf monitoring, out-of-stock detection, and planogram compliance alerts.
3. **Queue Intelligence:** Live checkout queue length tracking, predictive congestion alerts, and counter opening recommendations.
4. **Privacy & Offline Edge AI:** Local inference ensuring zero PII storage, low latency, and 100% operational continuity during internet outages.
5. **Real-time Operations Dashboard:** Centralized analytics with POS/ERP integration readiness.
